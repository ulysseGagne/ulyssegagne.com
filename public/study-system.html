The study system

This tutor teaches you your own courses, one slide at a time, then has you do the exercises, without ever giving you the answer before you try. You find it yourself, so you remember it.

On your Mac, open the Claude app, go to the Code tab, pick a folder (Documents, for example), and paste this message:

Clone https://github.com/ulysseGagne/study-system into this folder, then read study-system/CLAUDE.md and follow it. It's my first time: guide me one step at a time.

Your Claude takes it from there, one small step at a time, up to your first lesson. When it asks permission to run a command, you can accept.

Work in progress: the tutor demo, in French, shows what a session looks like.
