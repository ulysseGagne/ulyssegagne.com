Never enough

Why doing well never feels like enough, where that comes from, and the way out.

Dr. K (Dr. Alok Kanojia) is a psychiatrist trained at Harvard who, before medical school, spent years studying to become a monk. On his YouTube channel, HealthyGamerGG, he explains how the mind works, in plain words.
